# Vellum Real-Player Playtest Rubric

> Synchronized with [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) Section 14.6 & Section 17.3.
> Minimum requirement for V1 sign-off: 3–5 real players completing *Crimson Rain Manor* end-to-end.

## Session Metadata (Required for Every Playtest)
- Playtester alias & TRPG experience level (`newcomer` / `occasional` / `veteran`)
- Module version & experience mode used (`guided` / `classic` / `immersive`, note any mid-game switches)
- LLM provider + Proposal/Narration models + network region + hardware spec
- Total duration & turn count
- Hard-gate incidents (0 tolerated for release): rules error / secret leak / double charge / reload loss / save corruption

## Experience Questionnaire (1–5 Scale + Verbatim Notes)
1. **Scene clarity**: Did you always understand where you were and what was happening?
2. **Perceived freedom**: Did you feel able to attempt unscripted or creative actions?
3. **Player sovereignty**: Did the AI ever take over your character's thoughts, emotions, or major decisions?
4. **NPC believability**: Did NPCs feel distinct in voice, motive, and knowledge boundaries?
5. **Fairness of clues & secrets**: Did discoveries and dice outcomes feel earned and fair?
6. **Narrative pacing**: Where did prose feel bloated, repetitive, melodramatic, or flat?
7. **Latency & flow**: Did waiting, confirmations, or UI transitions ever break momentum?
8. **Dynamic views (DVP)**: Did inline dice, handouts, clocks, and the evidence board elevate immersion?
9. **Mode differentiation**: Did switching experience modes feel meaningfully different without changing fairness?
10. **Retention & replay intent**: Would you play another turn right now? Would you start a second scenario? Why or why not?
